## Lock 1 — Drawings before trades (owner 2026-08-20 ~14:52 · locked 2026-08-23)

**Owner, in substance:** We keep working like the world’s best tiler. We lay an amazing bathroom floor, then rip it up because nobody drew the pipes. A house is built because the team already knows how everything connects — wires, pipes, holes — **before** the specialist is brilliant. We ship a feature (Roblox block), then discover the other app needs a button, then another way. The full plan, schema, in-house owner experience, design, and architecture must be known so workers do what they know best. Not a 20-step phase list that discovers the house as we go.

**Grok completion the owner locked:**

1. This is **coordination**, not “foundation then walls then roof.”
2. Knowledge lives in the **set**, not in each worker’s head. The tiler does not have to understand the whole house. They work from drawings that already resolved the connections.
3. A numbered phase list is a **calendar of trades**. It is not the drawings. “Phase A: the loop. Phase B: add the switches” is tiling first, then remembering the pipes.
4. **“How will they use it?” is architecture.** Before code: which screen, which buttons, selected/highlight, waiting/time, the other app, empty, Off vs Pause vs Needs you.
5. **Hidden systems are pipes** (rules, VPN START, Always-On, FCM). If they are not on the drawing, the beautiful card is finish over a leak.
6. Research (what similar apps do, what they miss, what we already have) is the **brief**, before architecture — not a phase after we already tiled.
7. A phase after GO is a **slice of a finished drawing**, not a discovery. Missing button mid-build = we built without the set. Update the drawing, then the trade. Do not sneak a later trade into the current one.
8. **No first tile until the wet room is drawn** — living in it, every connected surface, every pipe — then trades in the sequence the drawing already forced.

**Forbidden:** starting a trade because we are skilled at it; calling a 20-step list “the plan”; discovering connected screens after ship.
